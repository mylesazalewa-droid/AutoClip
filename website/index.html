How it works

From the pulpit to your phone, without the editing night

  1. Sunday, after service

    The recording lands

    Save the service video to a folder AutoClip watches, or drop it in yourself.

  2. Sunday afternoon

    AutoClip finds the moments

    It transcribes on your Mac and builds clips with a strong opening, captions and verse cards.

  3. Sunday evening

    Context Guard checks them

    Clips that stop before “but”, or drop a “not”, are marked so you can fix them in one click.

  4. Monday morning

    You keep the best ones

    Swipe through: keep, toss, export. Nothing is ever posted for you.

Questions

Good to know

Does AutoClip post for me?

No. It makes and checks the clips. You choose what to post and where.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Transcription and the main clip picking run on your Mac. AI features such as writing hooks or the Scripture check use an API key you add, or on-device AI where your Mac supports it.

What Mac do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon is fastest for transcription.

Can I use clips of someone else's sermon?

Only with permission. Use recordings your church owns or has the rights to, including any music in them.

What happens when my trial or plan ends?

Everything you made stays. You can still open, edit and export it. New sermons need an active plan.
