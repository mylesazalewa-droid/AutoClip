AutoClip Help

Everything you need to go from a Sunday recording to clips you're glad to post.

Install

  1. Open AutoClip.dmg and drag AutoClip into Applications.
  2. Open AutoClip from Applications.
  3. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to AutoClip. You only do this once.
  4. When asked, allow AutoClip to use your Documents folder. That's where it keeps your clips.
  5. Download the speech model when prompted (about 230 MB, once). Or click See a finished example first to explore before downloading.

Your first sermon

  1. On Home, click Choose a sermon, or go to Clips → New Clip.
  2. Pick the service video. AutoClip transcribes it on your Mac and builds several clips.
  3. Click Quick review. Press → to keep a clip and ← to toss it.
  4. Export the ones you kept. They're saved as video files, with suggested captions where available.

Use recordings your church owns or has permission to use, including any music in them.

Editing a clip

Click Edit on any clip. Click a word to cut it, and click it again to bring it back. Untick a sentence to drop it.

Say what to change

Type in the box at the top of the editor, or press ⌘K. These work without AI:

  • remove the ums
  • start at the question
  • end on “stay in the room”
  • cut “these exact words”
  • make the captions bigger
  • make it square

Requests that need judgement, like “cut the part about my brother”, use AI if you've added a key.

The editor opens in its own window, so you can make it as big as you like and drag the line between the preview and the clips. Drag a clip by its ≡ handle to move it. The strip under the preview shows every clip, the hook, verse cards and movement; click it to jump. Apply to all clips (top of Polish) copies this clip's captions, hook animation, movement, size or sound to every clip from the sermon.

Context Guard

Context Guard looks for clips that might say something you didn't mean, such as a clip that stops just before “But…” or a cut that removes “not”. Most warnings have a one-click fix.

Check meaning and Scripture with AI reads the clip next to what was said around it and compares quoted verses with the translation you choose. AI can misremember Bible wording, so check the verse itself before relying on it.

Movement, hooks and series

In the editor's Polish section:

  • Hook animation sets how the opening text arrives: Pop, Slide in, Drop, Stamp or Wipe.
  • Movement adds a quick punch-in on the strongest lines and a slow push through long stretches, so a still shot doesn't feel flat.
  • Series: add the message to a sermon series, then Save as series look. New clips from that series get the same captions, hook animation and movement, and can show the series name and part number.

Uploading a whole service? Keep Find the sermon in a full service on when making clips. AutoClip listens for music, singing and talking and picks clips from the sermon only.

Finder, Dock and menu bar

  • Right-click a video in Finder and choose Quick Actions or Services → Make Clips with AutoClip. You can also drop a video on AutoClip's Dock icon.
  • The scissors in the menu bar shows what's being made and what's ready, and you can drop a video on it. Turn it off under View → Show in Menu Bar.
  • While clips are being made, the Dock icon shows progress. When they're ready and AutoClip is in the background, a notification offers Review.

Autopilot

  1. Click Autopilot at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Choose the folder your service recordings are saved to.
  3. Click Turn on Autopilot. Videos already in the folder are skipped.

New recordings become clips on their own and wait on Home for review. AutoClip has to be open and the Mac awake. Turn on Open AutoClip when this Mac starts to help with that. The daily limit stops a folder of old videos from running up an AI bill.

Volunteer Mode

  1. Set up captions, size and length the way you like in Clips.
  2. Click Volunteer mode in the sidebar, name the style and click Save style.
  3. Optionally set a PIN, then click Turn on Volunteer Mode.

Volunteers see three steps: add the sermon, pick the best clips, export. Use Share style to copy your style to another Mac. Volunteer Mode is on the Church plan.

Rehearse

Open Rehearse, then paste your notes, open a text file, or record a practice run. You'll see the likely length, lines that would open a clip well, and lines that lean on what came before. Ask AI what to strengthen gives specific suggestions in your own voice.

AI and API keys

AutoClip works without AI. Some extras, like writing hooks, Teasers and the Scripture check, use an AI service with your own API key:

  1. Create a key with Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or Google (Gemini).
  2. In AutoClip, choose AutoClip → Settings… (⌘,) → AI, pick the provider and paste the key.

The key is stored in your Mac's Keychain. The AI company bills you directly, and the AI chip in the sidebar shows an estimate of what you've spent. Set a spending limit in your AI account too.

Trial and license

The free trial makes clips from two sermons, with every feature. Trial exports carry a small “Made with AutoClip” mark. To activate, choose AutoClip → Activate AutoClip… and paste your key, or open the key file.

If a plan ends, everything you made stays. You can still open, edit and export it. New sermons need an active plan.

Updates

AutoClip checks for updates once a day. When one is ready, an Update button appears in the sidebar. Click it, then Install and reopen. Finish any clips in progress first. To check now, choose AutoClip → Check for Updates…

Light or dark

Choose View → Appearance, or Settings → General: Light, Dark, or Match Mac.

Problems

If AutoClip closes unexpectedly, it offers a report the next time it opens. You can read everything in it first, and nothing is sent unless you choose to. You can also choose Help → Report a Problem… at any time.

Nothing is posted to social media for you, and your videos stay on your Mac. AI features send what they need, usually the transcript, to the AI company you chose.
